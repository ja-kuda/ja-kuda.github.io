## What is Ambition?

Ambition is a narrative-driven pen & paper roleplaying game set on the continent of **Akudia**. Every character is driven by an ambition – the central leitmotif of their deepest ideals.

- Checks with d8 – every 5+ is a success, compared against the scale of conflict and effect
- Characters built from ambition, ancestry, skills, traits and talents
- Freeform arcane weaving, static spells and the elements of reality
