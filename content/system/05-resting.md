# Resting

A rest describes a night of full sleep or other recovery.

Resting allows various aspects of a character to regenerate and refresh. Certain talents also come into play during a rest.

The GM can decide that a rest does not allow all effects – for example, if the night's sleep was disturbed or the night was spent in a very inhospitable environment.

A full rest has these effects:

- One lvl 1 wound can be reduced by 1
- All charges of talents are refreshed, unless they have other conditions
- Weavers regain 6 of their spent threads
- Time-limited effects like "drunk" or "exhausted" are removed
- Certain talents trigger
