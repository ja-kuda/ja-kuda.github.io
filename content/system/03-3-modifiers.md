# Modifiers

A check can be changed by different kinds of modifiers.

### Boon
- A boon "enlarges" a die by one step, i.e. a d8 becomes a d10.
- By default a die can only be affected by one boon. If all dice in a check are already d10, further boons are lost.
- Boons can be granted temporarily, or used multiple times or permanently through a keyword.

### Flaw
- A flaw "shrinks" a die by one step, i.e. a d8 becomes a d6.
- A d6 is the smallest die that still has a chance of a success and cannot be modified further by flaws. Instead, other dice are modified until all flaws are distributed or there are no valid dice left in the check.

### Number of dice
- Certain effects can add d8 to or remove d8 from a check before rolling.
- lvl 3 wounds are an example: each removes one d8 from the check before rolling.

### Number of successes
- Certain effects can add successes to a check. They count in addition to the rolled result.
  - An effect that adds 1 success would turn a result of 0 successes into 1 success, pushing the scale from red to yellow.
- Conversely, negative effects can remove a number of rolled successes from the check before it is evaluated.
  - An effect that removes 1 success would turn a result of 2 successes into 1, changing the result from green to yellow.

### Rerolls
- Some effects allow rerolling one or more dice after a check has been rolled.
- This can be positive – rerolling dice that show no success – or negative, when successes already rolled have to be rerolled.

### Die faces
- Some effects bring special effects when a certain number is rolled.
