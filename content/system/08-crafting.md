# Crafting

The Crafting skill is deliberately kept very abstract.

It represents a character's basic understanding of mechanisms and tools, but also how good the character is with their hands.

In theory, every character – no matter how many points they have in Crafting – can try to make something. The system is meant to let you at least try any action.

Crafting differs from the other skills in that the difficulty for specific crafted items rises much faster and only becomes realistically usable with an investment in crafting talents.

This means the skill can still be used for general handiwork, while making alchemical poisons or magical items is reserved for characters who really specialize in it.
