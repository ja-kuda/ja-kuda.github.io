# Welcome to Ambition

**Ambition** is a narrative pen & paper roleplaying game set in the war-torn land of [Akudia](lore.html#welcome). Characters are driven by what they want most, every roll tells a story, and magic is as powerful as it is dangerous.

The rules stay light where they can and go deeper where it matters: the story comes first, and the dice decide *how* things turn out, not just *whether* they work.

> [!glance] At a glance
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Dice** | Roll a pool of d8, increase or decrease some of them to d10 or d6 |
> | **Core roll** | A single check per action, read on a scale of **conflict** and **effect** |
> | **Characters** | Built around an **ambition**, with skills, talents, traits and personal arcs |
> | **Magic** | Freeform weaving with threads, fixed static spells and necromancy |
> | **Health** | Wounds in four levels instead of hit points |
> | **Play style** | Story first – positions, consequences and choices rather than exact numbers |

---

## What to expect

- **Characters with a drive.** Every character has an [ambition](#ambition) – a single idea that sums up what they live for. Acting on it earns **resolve**, which lets you reroll checks or fuel talents.
- **Personal stories.** [Arcs](#arcs) give each character their own quest next to the group's adventure.
- **Outcomes with shades of grey.** A roll rarely just succeeds or fails. You often get what you want only at a price.
- **Magic that pushes back.** Freeform magic can do almost anything, but every thread you pull thins the fabric of reality, and the weaver pays for it.
- **Tough, lasting consequences.** Wounds weigh on your checks, and facing death is a dramatic choice, not just a number dropping to zero.

## The core mechanics

1. **Describe what you do.** The GM sets the [position](#position) – from *safe* to *absurd* – and with it the **target**: the number of successes you need.
2. **Roll your dice.** Your [skill](#skills) gives you d8s. Every die showing 5+ is a success.
3. **Read the scale.** Below, on or above the target decides how much **[conflict](#conflict-effect)** (consequences) and **effect** (progress towards your goal) you get.

The [scale](#position) at a glance:

| Below target | Target | Above target |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| **+Conflict · −Effect** | **Conflict · Effect** | **−Conflict · +Effect** |

Players can negotiate their position and take a bigger risk for more effect, or play it safe and give up some of it.

> [!tip] Magic works the same way
> A [spell check](#arcane-weaving) uses the same scale. Instead of a skill, the weaver spends **[threads](#threads)** for dice. The more threads they pull, the harsher the feedback.

## Why Ambition

- **One simple roll for everything** – fights, talks, sneaking and spells all use the same check and the same scale.
- **Every roll moves the story** – conflict and effect give the GM and players something to play with, even on a bad roll.
- **Freeform magic with real stakes** – describe any spell you like, then decide how much you are willing to risk.
- **Items by role, not by stats** – a sword, a dagger and a bow differ in *how* they are used, not in damage numbers. See [Inventory](#inventory).
- **Deep but optional** – talents, ancestries and aspects of the gods add plenty of options without slowing the table down.
