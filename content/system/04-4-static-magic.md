# Static magic

Static magic is the second major form of magic in Ambition.

It is the more scientific approach to casting spells and manipulating the fabric of existence.

Static magic describes and covers all spells that have been "encapsulated": their effects were isolated over time and refined so far that they still need threads to be cast, but no longer tear the fabric of existence.

The threads are only borrowed – after the effect has run its course, they weave back into their natural structure.

The advantage is that a static mage doesn't have to worry about the effect failing, harming them, or otherwise becoming dangerous to them and their surroundings.

In exchange, they are more limited in their choice of magic: static spells usually have a precisely defined function and reproducible effects.

Static spells are still magic, though, and require complex formulas and rituals to be performed successfully. A static mage will spend a large part of their life finding and learning spells.

Beyond a certain point, static mages tend to carry a collection of formulas and rituals they can use to draw on different spells.

## Static spells in play

In Ambition, static spells are noted as additional talents on the character sheet – see the list of [static spells](#static-spells).

A normal character can choose **up to 3 static spells** as regular talents. The character can cast these from memory.

If a character should master more than these 3 spells, they can choose talents that go deeper in this direction and allow learning a multitude of static spells.

Static spells cost threads, but the negative effects of threads do not apply to static spells.

This means a character who only knows static spells can cast up to 9 spells per day.

A weaver can also choose to cast static spells, but these increase the risk of gaining more conflict when casting an arcane spell.
