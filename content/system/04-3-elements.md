# Elements

The fabric of reality follows certain rules and structures.

It is made up of a number of elements that work together to form reality.

Each of these elements can be isolated and changed by arcane weavers, but not every mage can master every element.

With few exceptions, an arcane weaver controls a single element – and often not in its entirety, but in one of its forms.

## Secondary and tertiary elements

Most elements are related to two or four other elements. These are called secondary or tertiary elements.

In certain cases a creature can learn to control some of these connected elements in addition to their main element.
