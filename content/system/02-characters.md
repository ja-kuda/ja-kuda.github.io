# Characters

A character in Ambition is made up of the following parts:

- **[Ambition](#ambition)** – the central driving force of the character.
- **[Arcs](#arcs)** – abstract character quests. Completing one raises the resolve limit by 1.
- **[Ancestry](#ancestry)** – sets the ancestry talents and wounds.
- **Resolve** – can be used to reroll a check or to pay for talents that list resolve as their cost.
- **Traits** – can give boons or flaws on checks, depending on the trait and the kind of check.
- **[Talents](#talents)** – the special abilities of the characters.
  - Different kinds of tags sort the talents.
  - They can have a number of uses per day.
  - They can have different costs.
  - They can have certain prerequisites.

> [!tip] Build a character
> The [character builder](characters.html#builder) walks you through [character creation](#character-creation) step by step, and the [talent builder](characters.html#talents) lets you browse and plan talents.
