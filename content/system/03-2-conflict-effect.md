# Conflict and effect

Every check has two results, which turn out stronger or weaker depending on the number of successes.

## Conflict

*Consequences of a situation for the character.*

Conflict is the negative or unforeseen influence on an action.

The result of the check decides the severity of the conflict, the GM decides the kind of conflict – though it can be interesting to give the player a choice.

Conflict can, for example, be:

- Physical damage
- Losing or damaging items
- Loss of time
- An unfavorable situation for the next check
- Reduced effect

## Effect

*The character's impact on the world and the story.*

Effect describes how successful a character was with their action, or how far the situation met their expectations.

Based on the effect, the character ends up in a new starting situation and plans and carries out their next actions from there.

The stronger the effect, the closer the character gets to their goal. A few examples:

- **In a fight**, strong effect means the opponent is badly wounded, normal effect is a weak hit and weak effect only a graze.
- **In a time-limited situation**, stronger effect means actions are carried out faster, weak effect means more lost time.
- **For a spell**, strong effect could mean the effect lasts longer or is especially potent.
- **In social situations**, strong effect could mean the other person is less suspicious, even if they agree at first.

Effect and conflict always influence each other – conflict tends to affect the immediate consequences, effect influences future actions.
