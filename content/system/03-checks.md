# Checks

Whenever the outcome of a situation is uncertain or comes with certain risks, a check is rolled in Ambition.

A check always consists of a skill (or keyword) and a difficulty.

The skill or keyword states which source of dice is used, while the difficulty determines the evaluation on the [scale of success](#position).

The base dice for all checks in Ambition are d8 – eight-sided dice.

Most checks made in this game are based on skills or on arcane magic.

All checks start with 1d8 as their base. The number of dice can be increased or reduced by skills, chaos, wounds and so on.

However, a check can never have fewer than 1 die – the smallest possible check is 1d6.

When making a check, the dice are rolled and every die showing **5 or more** counts as a **success**.

The successes are added up and compared to the difficulty.

The standard difficulty of a check is 1. The GM can raise or lower it due to a variety of factors to create an exciting story.

## Basic procedure

1. The player describes an action they want to take.
2. The GM sets the difficulty (0, 1, 2, 3+) and the required skill.
   1. The player can argue for a different skill.

## Distribution of 3d8

With 3d8, the expected average, the number of successes is distributed like this (every die has a 50 % chance of a success):

| Successes | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Chance | 12.5 % | 37.5 % | 37.5 % | 12.5 % |
