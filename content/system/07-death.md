# Death

A character faces death when they receive a **lvl 4 wound** – either:

- directly (this should only be used very, very rarely), or
- when they receive a lower-level wound while all their wounds are already filled.

The player then chooses between a **heroic stand** and a **bargain**.

## Heroic stand
- The character accepts death.
- They then take one final action, which can be especially powerful.

## Bargain
- A deal with a god, a patron or similar – basically a deal with the GM.
- The character stays alive, but unconscious, and gains a permanent effect.
