# Talents

Talents are the special abilities of characters.

While skills are used for all kinds of actions within a normal scope, talents allow characters to break that scope and make them truly unique.

Talents are distributed during character creation and can also be gained in different ways over the course of a campaign.

Every talent is described with these fields:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The name of the talent |
| **Tags** | Ancestry, archetype, god or spell group |
| **Uses** | How often the talent can be used per day (refreshed by a [rest](#resting)) |
| **Cost** | What has to be paid to use it |
| **Prerequisites** | Skills, talents or other conditions needed to take it |
| **Effects** | What the talent does |

## Kinds of talents

- **[Ancestry talents](#ancestry-talents)** – granted by your ancestry
- **[Talents by archetype](#archetype-talents)** – the main pool, sorted by archetype
- **[Aspect talents](#aspect-talents)** – gifts of the gods
- **[Static spells](#static-spells)** – fixed spells
