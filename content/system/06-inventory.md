# Inventory

A character's inventory and the items they own and use are mainly defined by their role in the fiction.

This means most items don't have concrete stats, as you might know them from other roleplaying games. Instead, items differ in their role in different situations and positions.

A sword, a dagger and a bow, for example, are basically "equally strong" when a character wants to attack a creature.

They differ in their role: a sword is more useful in an open fight, a dagger for unnoticed use, and a bow for ranged attacks.

A crowbar, a lockpick and a magic potion can all play the same role when it comes to opening a door – they differ mainly in the approach and the kind of action.

## Special items

Of course there are also special items. They stand out through two aspects:

- **Traits** – special items can have one or more traits that can become boons or flaws in certain situations.
- **Talents** – some items, especially magical or otherwise special ones, can bring their own abilities connected to using the item. These are noted just like a character's talents, which makes handling special weapons or other items easier.
