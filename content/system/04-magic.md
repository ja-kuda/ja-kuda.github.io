# Magic

There are three forms of magic in Ambition:

- **[Arcane weaving](#arcane-weaving)** – freeform magic, the most flexible but also the riskiest form of magic
- **[Static magic](#static-magic)** – fixed spells with defined effects and costs
- **Necromancy** – part of freeform magic, using the Life element, with a few special rules

Arcane magic draws on the [elements](#elements) and on [threads](#threads) pulled from the fabric of reality.
