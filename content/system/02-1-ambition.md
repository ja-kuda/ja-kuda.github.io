# Ambition

The ambition is the central driving force of every character.

It covers the character's general attitude towards life and problem solving, and reflects the basic character idea and their deep-seated needs and desires.

Although an ambition should be chosen when creating a character, it does not have to be unchangeable or all-encompassing.

A character should not be a slave to their ambition – they can still act and decide freely. The ambition should rather reflect the core idea of the character.

So it is entirely possible to first build a complete character, think about their traits and choose a fitting ambition at the end – or to pick an interesting ambition and build the character around it.

Ideally, an ambition can be summed up in a single word and describes a certain concept of the character.
