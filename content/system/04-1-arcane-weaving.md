# Arcane weaving

Arcane magic is one of the three forms of magic characters can use.

It is Ambition's freeform magic – the most flexible, but also the riskiest.

With arcane magic (or weaver magic), no fixed spells are cast as with static magic. Instead, just like other checks, a target is set based on the situation and the action played out (in this case, casting a certain spell), and a check determines how strong conflict and effect turn out.

Unlike normal checks, there is no dedicated skill that sets how many base dice can be used for arcane magic.

Instead, the character draws on a resource called **[threads](#threads)**, which adds further dice to their spell check.

## Casting a freeform spell

1. The character describes their spell and the desired effect.
2. The GM determines the situation, just like for normal checks.
   - Here too, the player can negotiate – see the [Checks](#checks) chapter.
3. The GM sets the target of the check.
4. The player spends a number of threads to get their base dice for the spell check.
   - The player must spend at least 1 thread as soon as the spell has a noticeable effect.
   - Trivial tasks – lighting a campfire, slightly warming or cooling something, creating a brief small light or similar effects that only matter for roleplay and wouldn't need a roll – can always be cast without spending threads.
5. The player rolls their dice for the check.
   - Any modifiers and effects for spell checks apply here.
6. Based on the result, effect and conflict are evaluated as with normal checks.
7. The conflict is adjusted based on the thread track.
8. The character suffers any conflict, gains any effect and finds themselves in a new situation.
