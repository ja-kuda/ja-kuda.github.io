# Key terms

A quick overview of the most important terms in Ambition.

### Ambition
An ambition is the central leitmotif of a character and the most abstract representation of that character's deepest ideals. Every character has an ambition.

### Arcs
An arc is a personal quest a character chooses for themselves at certain times during a campaign or during character creation. Arcs do not have to be completed – they can be used to guide a character's growth or personal storyline. Finishing a quest increases the maximum resolve for everyone in the party.

### Checks
Every time a character takes an action whose outcome is uncertain, they make a check – the central action players take in this system to determine their character's fate. A check is made with a number of base dice (d8), which can be altered or modified through a number of effects and abilities.

### Position
Every time a check is called, the characters in question are in a specific position. This situation determines their position in the fiction and influences the outcome of their actions.

### Target
The target is the number of successes a character needs to roll in a check to gain a positive outcome. It is mostly determined by the situation, or by the player haggling for a different number with different possible outcomes.

### Scale
The scale is a quick way to determine the outcome of a check based on the target. It shows the resulting conflict and effect.

### Conflict
Based on the check's result on the scale, the character gains conflict – negative or unforeseen consequences of their actions. Conflict ranges from mild inconveniences to severe harm, depending on the situation and the result.

### Effect
Based on the check's result on the scale, the character has an impact on the fiction and on achieving their goals. Effect determines to what extent the character achieved what they set out to do – from barely achieving an idea of the original plan to excelling at the task.

### Resolve
Resolve is a meta currency that can be used either for certain abilities or to reroll checks. Resolve can only be gained by acting on a character's ambition or by having an interesting impact on the story. The maximum resolve can be increased over time.

### Spell check
Similar to a normal check, a spell check is made when a character wants to cast – or weave – a freeform spell. The number of dice is not based on a skill, but on the number of threads the character spends.

### Threads
Arcane threads represent the maximum number of dice a character can use in spell checks. The number of threads currently in use also determines the conflict gained from casting arcane spells.

### Static magic
A different type of magic where spells are not created dynamically, but are specific effects with specific costs.

### Necromancy
The third type of magic. Although it falls under freeform magic, necromancy has a few special rules.

### Skills
Skills describe a character's abilities in specific areas, split into physical, mental and social groups. A skill ranges from 0 to 5 – the higher, the more proficient the character is.

### Talents
Talents are the special abilities of characters and can have wildly differing effects and combinations.

### Wounds
Wounds represent the physical and mental health of a character. They are filled when a character takes damage and can have negative effects on checks or even kill a character.
