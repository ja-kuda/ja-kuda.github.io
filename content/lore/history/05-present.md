## How the war began

- **Rogamon returns** – Around year 225, a group of cultists brings the Necromancer King back to life. This time a group of unremarkable heroes stops the still weakened necromancer before he gets further than the destruction of Winterglehn and its surroundings.
- **The Empire prepares** – Emperor **Santor** sees the events in Varland as proof that magic must finally be brought under control and begins to plan a war against "the magical kingdom". When he uncovers a shadow organisation within his own Empire, he disappears; **Suta** takes his place.
- **Varland's ruin** – Expecting the invasion, **King Anselm I** of Varland, out of fear and mistrust of his own vassals, pulls back to the heart of his kingdom and casts off the outer provinces – precisely those that will feel the invasion first. All military power is gathered in the Partition around Hammerfall.
- **The realms break apart** – **Weißküst** and the **Rotmark** join as the **Red-White Alliance** to defend themselves. The **Expansion** and **Silbertal** become the independent **New Expansion** under a nameless warlord. The Durag of **Bregonia** reject Anselm's rule and found their own kingdom.
- **The offensive** – Around year 234, the Tralian Empire launches a new offensive. A great war now spreads across Akudia.

> [!secret] Hidden truth
> Emperor Santor did not simply disappear: the **Machinarium** had him removed when he began to investigate its leadership, and installed Suta as its puppet – without Suta himself knowing. See [Factions and Organisations](#factions).
