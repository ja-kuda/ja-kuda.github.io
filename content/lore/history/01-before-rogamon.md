# Before Rogamon (vKR)

<div data-embed="era" data-era="before-rogamon"></div>

After the God Wars, Akudia was one of the few places where life remained. Elves, Durag, Korna and other peoples retreated here, while humans stayed behind in the ruins of the old realms far away.

## The rule of the elves

A few centuries after the survivors settled, the **elves** seized control of Akudia. As conventional war was hardly possible with so few people left, they turned to their biological and alchemical knowledge: they created creatures and monsters and above all **the Blights** – diseases and curses they used to oppress the other peoples, before sealing them away because of their danger.

## The arrival of the humans

Over centuries, the few human enclaves that survived the God Wars built new civilisations and spread to every corner of the world that was halfway habitable. Eventually humans arrived on Akudia and began to settle it. Elves and humans lived together peacefully at first and even shared territories and realms.

## The Elf Wars

Then something happened that marked the beginning of the end for the elves. They lost their immunity to disease, then their immortality. Elven souls never truly die – they are reborn in a new elf – but now rebirth failed. Many of the dying elves blamed the humans.

Mistrust turned to open hostility and ended in the **Elf Wars**, the greatest war since the age of the gods. The elves even released the Blights, sealed for thousands of years – but it was not enough. In the end they were too few and too scattered; only a few escaped death by following the call of **Corvilas** deep underground.

## The humans take Akudia

Soon after the Elf Wars, humans brought the whole continent under their control. The remaining small realms of the Durag, the Korna and others were destroyed, driven away or annexed. The Korna were nearly wiped out, and the survivors fled into exile in the far north; the Durag withdrew deep underground or to **Bregonia**. Around this time the first **Orvosa** settled on Akudia – pushed to the edges of society even then, which in the centre of the continent has lasted to this day.

## The division of the continent

With the whole continent in their hands, humans began to fight each other for land, resources, power and wealth. Many small wars, alliances and betrayals followed, until two realms emerged that divided the continent between them: the **Great Kingdom of Varland** in the north and the **Free Alliance of Atania** in the south. The *First Accord* set the border between them.

Varland was soon divided into regions ruled by lords, as it had grown too large to be governed from one place. Atania was ruled not by a royal house but by its city-states, which gained more and more self-government through the *Second Accord*. A long age of growth and prosperity followed – the **Golden Times**.

## The rise of necromancy

Although the balance between life and death had been damaged beyond repair when Visaron died, it took thousands of years until it showed in mortals. Then children were born with **necromantic abilities** – able to control the energies of life and death and even the undead, a dangerous counterpart to the arcane mages.

The **Lord of Tears**, the first necromancer, taught the gift for good: farmers used it to let the remains of their animals work, mourners spoke with the dead one last time. But the lords and the crown – many of them mages themselves – felt threatened. Children with the talent were taken from their families, locked away, tortured and used for experiments.

One of these children was **Rogamon**.
