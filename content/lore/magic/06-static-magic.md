# Static magic

Static magic is the more scientific approach to manipulating the fabric of existence. Its spells were "encapsulated": their effects were isolated over time and refined so far that they still need threads, but no longer tear the fabric. The threads are only borrowed – after the effect has run its course, they weave back into their natural structure.

Static mages spend a large part of their lives finding and learning spells, and tend to carry collections of formulas and rituals.
