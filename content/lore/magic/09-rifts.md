# The Rifts

When the fabric of reality tears, a **Rift** opens. The first great rifts appeared in the God Wars, when reality shattered into the **mirror realities**: rifts between the mirror worlds opened and closed and took hundreds of thousands of lives.

Traces of those times remain on Akudia:

- **The Scars** near Beltheim – gorges full of glowing pink crystals, said to have been created in a battle between two gods. The crystals amplify magic and spread with every arcane explosion.
- **Feitor**, a quiet village near the Salt Road – the small forest nearby is known for regular rifts into the **Feywild**.
