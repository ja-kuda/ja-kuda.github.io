# The fabric of reality

Before life and structure existed, there was only the [Null](#null). Then the Null tore open – an event known as **the Tearing** – and exposed the **weave**, a mass of energies and threads that began to form structures and existence. From these structures, reality as we know it was woven: the world, land, time, space and life.

The existence in which Ambition takes place is shielded from the Null by this **fabric of reality**, which allows structures to form and life to flourish. The fabric follows certain rules and structures. It is made up of a number of [elements](#elements) that work together to form reality. Normally it is stable and impenetrable to the Null.

## The weave

Some beings could sense the weave behind existence, and a few learned to communicate with it and control parts of it – the first mages, and later [the gods](#ascension). Since the god **Arkas** killed himself to escape his ascension and changed the weave, far more beings are born with arcane abilities.

The weave is not equally strong everywhere. In **Winters End** in the far north it is especially strong, and mages there gain abilities that would be impossible elsewhere.

## The mirror realities

The God Wars shook the weave so badly that reality broke into thousands of shards – the **mirror realities**. Rifts between these mirror worlds opened and closed, taking hundreds of thousands of lives. See [The Rifts](#rifts).
