# Magic in everyday life

How people see magic depends very much on where they live.

> [!glance] At a glance
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Varland** | Mages were long respected and formed much of the nobility; today fear of magic is widespread, but it is not banned |
> | **Tralian Empire** | Free use of magic is forbidden and harshly punished |
> | **Beltheim and the Scars** | Any magic is forbidden because of the unstable crystals |
> | **Winters End** | The weave is strongest here – the home of the academy Ars Arkanum |
> | **Necromancy** | Feared and hunted almost everywhere since Rogamon |

## Varland

In the Great Kingdom of Varland, mages and arcanists were highly respected before Rogamon, and their abilities often brought them positions of power – much of the nobility were mages. Rogamon almost wiped out this nobility. After his reign, fear of necromancy and magic ruled the population for a long time. Today some cities, like **Weißstein**, practise magic openly again, but real mages are still rare.

Wealthy lords and ladies send their children on the long, dangerous journey to the academy **Ars Arkanum** in Winters End as soon as they show arcane abilities – the prestige of a successful graduation is worth the risk.

The **Arcane Sanctum**, an association of mages, investigates a mysterious **illness** that is affecting more and more arcanists.

## The Tralian Empire

Tralien rejected the use of magic long before the Empire existed. Today the free use of any magic is forbidden. Mages found by the **Grey Guard** and judged sane are drafted into the army and trained there; those judged unsafe are killed or sent to *re-education camps* from which they do not return. Propaganda makes people betray even their oldest friends and families.

Emperor **Lartarte** loosened the persecution considerably and began to use magic in certain areas of the Empire, but the deep fear has only partly faded. **Riewenfall**, "the magical city", is home to a comparatively large number of mages; in the **Border Fields**, mages are not actively hunted at all.

## Beltheim and the Scars

The crystals of the Scars amplify any magic around them – and make it unstable. To prevent accidents, any magic is forbidden in Beltheim and harshly punished by the Inquisition and its **Arcane Guard**.
