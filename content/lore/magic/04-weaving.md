# Threads and weaving

When arcane mages begin to manipulate the fabric of reality and restructure it for their purposes, it loses structure and hold. Single threads come loose – exactly those threads that the arcane weavers bring into new forms.

And as these threads are pulled out, the border between reality and the [Null](#null) blurs. The more threads are pulled, the more arcane magic is worked in one place, the thinner the fabric becomes – and in the worst case it can tear, often with devastating consequences.

## Arcane scars

Arcane scars mark a mage as an arcane weaver and always depend on the element used.

> [!example]
> A lightning mage might receive a scar that twitches with electricity, a time mage a scar that looks aged, or an essence mage a scar that looks scaly or like bark.
