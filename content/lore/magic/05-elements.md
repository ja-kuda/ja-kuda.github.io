# The elements

The fabric of reality is made up of **elements** – the building blocks a [weaver](#weaving) isolates and reshapes. Each element can be changed by arcane magic, but not every mage can master every element: with few exceptions, a weaver controls a single element – and often only one of its forms.

One element stands apart: **Life**, the element of souls and the dead, is the root of [necromancy](#necromancy).
