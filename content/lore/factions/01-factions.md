# Factions and Organisations

Groups that act across borders – orders, guilds, churches, secret societies and pirates.

---

## In Varland

### The 13 Faithful
A group of mighty warriors, mages and others who fought for justice against King Rogamon. Founded and led by **Lord Rabenhorst**, they finally defeated the Necromancer King at the cost of their lives. Their deed is sung about in the [Ballad of the 13 Faithful](#legends).

### The Paladins of the Good
Founded by Lord Rabenhorst to resist Rogamon. The small order of paladins that had its seat in **Old Rybben** before Rogamon was wiped out with the city; Lord Rabenhorst rebuilt it more than a hundred years later in the neighbouring town of Rabenhorst.

### The necromancer hunters
Founded after the age of necromancy to track down and destroy dark magic. Their guild has its seat in the Partition.

### The Inquisition of Beltheim
Rules Beltheim and the Scars with an iron hand on behalf of the king, led by **High Inquisitor Horuas**. Its **Arcane Guard** protects the city alongside the normal guard; using magic is a serious crime.

### The Arcane Sanctum
An association of mages and those knowledgeable in magic from Varland. Their goal: to investigate the mysterious illness that is affecting more and more arcanists and stop it from spreading.

### The Rightwalkers
A group of adventurers led by **Darion Sterling**, who set out to right injustices and help the poor and helpless.

### The Children of the New Morning
The most devoted followers of **Saranah**, mostly people who had already given up hope. Born during Rogamon's reign, the community still grows in times of war, hunger and catastrophe.

### The Conclave
A group of extremely powerful mages, necromancers and other power-hungry individuals.

> [!secret] The Conclave's plan
> The Conclave wants to free King Rogamon from his prison and bring him back to life, plunging the world into a new age of necromancy and chaos. They hope that Rogamon's power will pass to them after his resurrection, so they can rule at his side. To bring him back, they search for information on a ritual and stir chaos – conflict and bloodshed make his return easier.
>
> Its members are named after the sins: **Pride** (Malek, the mastermind, infiltrating the king's government), **Greed** (an old cursed mage collecting artefacts), **Lust** (Lady Kamina, recruiting followers and stirring unrest), **Sloth** (Jekkel, a spy at the academy), **Wrath** (a failed mage turned undead, bound to Malek), **Gluttony** (Magdus Bellorum, who absorbs magic and destroys knowledge about Rogamon's defeat) and **Envy** (Lord Varano Dynaris, a young vampire).

### The Ice Warriors
A war band of Korna, half-giants and other warlike individuals in the Ice Desert, founded by Morg Urschlag and today led by **Marinesh Stormfist**.

### The Raiders
Viking-like groups in the southern Ice Desert who terrorise Varland's northern coasts by ship. The Storm Keep is still manned to hold them and other dangers from the north back on land.

## In the Tralian Empire

### The Grey Guard
The elite anti-magic unit of the imperial military: few, extremely well-trained soldiers and specialists. It mostly operates within the Empire, but some units were used in the occupation of the Rotmark to counter magical defences.

### The Machinarium
Officially, a secret organisation that once developed new war machines.

> [!secret] The truth about the Machinarium
> Founded in strict secrecy by **Norman Pastell** before he became emperor, the Machinarium has turned into a cult that wants to force the evolution of humanity by uniting machine and arcane. Its leaders, the **three High Priests**, secretly control the entire military. Their final goal is to isolate and destroy the weave, banish magic from the world and begin an age of machines. They had Emperor **Santor** removed when he began to investigate them, and installed **Suta** as emperor – without Suta knowing.

## In the south and at sea

### The Gold Chains
Several mercenary companies in the service of the **Sea Lords** of the Summerlight Coast – a kind of private army for protection and for keeping order in the city-states. Recognisable by the golden chains on their armour and weapons.

### The First Answer
A guild of medically gifted and greedy mercenaries. Anyone who can afford them pays a monthly fee – and in case of injury, they rescue and heal their client as fast as possible, regardless of losses.

### The Ivory League
The government of the Splinter Islands – an alliance of powerful merchant guilds and cities on the scattered islands.

### The Brotherhood of Irons Crossing
An alliance of pirate crews sworn to **Irons Crossing**, protecting their freedom under the **Pirate King Sulden**. Independent pirates have no protection and no territory – but no master either.

## Cults

### The Raven who cleanses the world
After the end of the Conclave of old, the remains of the **Cabal of Korvus** turned to a new master: the Raven who will cleanse the world. This mystical being is said to herald the end of civilisation as it is known and free the world from the carrion of humanity. No one knows exactly who or what it is; its followers are always searching for signs.
