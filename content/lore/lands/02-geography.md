# Geography

Akudia stretches over many climate zones – from the eternal ice in the north to the hungering sands in the south. Individual places are described on the [map](map.html).

> [!glance] At a glance
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Far north** | The Ice Desert, the Quivering Woods, Winters End |
> | **North** | The Dragon Vale and the Dragon's Neck, the island of Orubus |
> | **Heartland** | The Partition with Hammerfall and the Scars, the plains of Triften |
> | **West** | The Expansion with Winterglehn, Silbertal, the Fields of Silence, Bregonia |
> | **South-west** | Weißküst and its white cliffs, the Rotmark |
> | **Centre** | Tralien, the island of Atikun, the Tralian Wilderness |
> | **South** | The Border Fields, Atania, the Splinter Islands off the coast |

## The Ice Desert

The largest and most hostile region of the north. As far as the eye can see, ice and snow cover the ground; constant storms hide the distance, and in the far north even the sea is frozen. It was never settled or governed.

- **The Quivering Woods** (Zitterwälder) in the south-east, at the only land bridge to the rest of the continent, offer some shelter from the elements. Viking-like villages lie on the southern coast; deep inside live yetis, shadow wolves and frost elementals.
- **Winters End**, at the top of a cliff above the frozen sea, looks deserted from afar – but life goes on inside the stone houses and the tunnels between them. The **weave** is especially strong here, which makes mages unusually powerful. The ancient academy **Ars Arkanum** trains mages here, far from the distractions of the world; its grandmasters rule the region regardless of the king's wishes.
- Nomadic tribes roam the ice – among them Korna clans whose ancestors survived the persecution. Half-giant tribes on the southern coasts still follow the old ways. The **Raiders** terrorise Varland's northern coasts by ship.

## The Dragon Vale

A region of high mountains and the only natural passage to the far north. The south of the **Dragon's Neck** is picturesque, full of small mountain villages and pastures. Further north, the **Pass Watch** guards the last pass through the mountains, which ends at the **Storm Keep** – a reminder of the war that drove the Korna out of the region. The dragons, they say, have not been seen in generations.

## Orubus

A wild island north of the Partition, almost completely covered in forest and barely settled – a dream for hunters, and infamous as the home of wendigos, skinwalkers and other mythical monsters. Orubus is the home of the **druids** and shape-shifters, and the legendary **skalds** are said to come from here. The only town is **Rocham**. The island is practically lawless; Rocham answers to the crown only in paying taxes.

## The Partition

The heart of Varland, divided by the mountain range **Schnitter** in the north and the river **Endwasser** in the south. Mountains, great rivers, waterfalls and hills shape the land. The capital **Hammerfall** sits on a height between two arms of the Endwasser, the king's palace right above the waterfalls.

**The Scars** in the north-east are a region of cracks, craters and gorges full of glowing pink **crystals** – created, legend says, in a battle between two gods in the last of the God Wars. The crystals amplify magic many times over, but make it unstable and prone to arcane explosions. The city of **Beltheim** is built into the cliffs above them; its archive is the largest library on the continent.

## Triften

The westernmost of Varland's inner regions: wide plains with hills and groves. Before Rogamon it was covered in rich fields and orchards; his dark magic drained the land, and the descendants of the deformed creatures he created still roam it. Travellers are advised to stick to the roads and the few towns. The only larger town is **Rabenhorst**.

## The Expansion and Winterglehn

The Expansion stretches from Varland's north-west coast over the Fields of Silence to Triften and Silbertal – fields, forests and many villages along the **Salt Road**, one of the most important trade roads of the continent. **Winterglehn**, the largest city on the continent, stands on the ruins of Old Winterglehn: a centre of trade, a maze of alleys – and of crime and corruption. **Zweigheim**, on both sides of the river Siechlauf, guards one of the few crossings.

## The Fields of Silence

A gigantic grassland between the **Siechberge** and **Rogamon's Back**, reached through a pass in the south. Once the **Golden Valley**, today it lies in eternal dusk under a sky that always promises a storm that never comes. Every sound is swallowed. The fields are considered cursed and are avoided by almost everyone. In the north lies **Driftholz**, a ruined town sinking into a moor.

## Silbertal

A valley between the Silberling and the Brachtung, named for its silver – quiet forests, small villages, picturesque streams. On closer look: abandoned villages, fallow fields and deserted mines, late consequences of Rogamon's rule and the economic crisis after the last silver rush. The **Hexenwald**, a dense forest near Kilrum, has been considered cursed since an accident in a nearby mine; animals mutate and mages never tire there.

## Bregonia

A rock island off the coast of Silbertal, rising from the Evening Sea like a gigantic iceberg – the last bastion of the Durag. Their capital **Brung**, the "great cauldron", lies inside the highest, hollow mountain. Stone wyverns, trolls and earth elementals roam the barren land; ghosts of forgotten Durag haunt the catacombs.

## Weißküst

Varland's south-western coast, named after its high white limestone cliffs, which form a natural wall against the Tralian Empire. Most of the land lies high above the sea and is covered by the **Laughing Woods**. Weißküst is the wealthiest region of Varland, thanks to its resources and its crafting cities **Weißstein** and **Westkliff**.

## The Rotmark

The border march in Varland's south, framed by the Endwasser in the north and the Rotmark mountains, the Border Mountains and the **Blue Arm** in the south. Battle after battle was fought on its fields; the blood-soaked plains and shallow graves, circled by crows, gave the region its name. Its fortress towns **Steilfurt**, **Mondburg** and **Windwart** guard the border.

## Tralien and Atikun

**Tralien**, once one of Varland's largest provinces, is the heart of the Tralian Empire. **Central Tralien** has a perfect climate for wine and fruit and many wealthy towns – **Riewenfall**, "the magical city" by its waterfalls, **Champ de Soleil**, the city where no one stops, and others. The **Stahlmark** runs along the southern coast of the Krabbenarm. **Atikun**, an island in the Evening Sea, is rich in gold – rolling green hills, fog banks and steep coasts – and home to the imperial capital **Goldzahn**.

## The Tralian Wilderness and the Border Fields

The **Tralian Wilderness** in the east of the Empire is a land of moors, treacherous forests and monsters, sparsely settled and only nominally under imperial control. The **Border Fields** in the south are an endless steppe of dry grass, ruined trading posts overrun by beasts – a land of smugglers, bandits and bounty hunters, and one of the few places in the Empire where mages are not actively hunted.

## The south

The **Free Alliance of Atania** spans the south of the continent: the **Summerlight Coast**, the **Suncourt Basin** (separated by the river **Gamberi**), **Kalagar**, **Ardano**, the **Emerald Gardens**, the **Mattwald** and the **Hungering Sands**. *Placeholder – details to come.*

## The Splinter Islands

Not a land of their own but countless islands, atolls and archipelagos in the Evening Sea, off the south-west of the continent. There is no single government – each island is often controlled by a different organisation, and pirate fleets fight for control.
