A famous stud farm with a small town in the east of Weißküst, a little off the Salt Road, surrounded by picturesque woods and wide meadows. It breeds and trains the best horses on the continent. About 750 people live here, most of them working with the stud or the land. Twice a year a great fair draws buyers from all over the world.

## Rogamon's Reign

Founded by **Kantor Warren** to protect horses from the undead legions. Many of the first inhabitants settled here in gratitude and helped build the stud.

## Present

**Lord Ludwig Warren** rules the stud, the town and the lands around it – there has always been a Warren in charge, making the family one of the oldest in Varland. Its horses now go to the armies of the [Red-White Alliance](lore.html?realm=red-white-alliance#realms).
