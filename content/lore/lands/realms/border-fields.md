The **Border Fields** (Grenzfelder) are a plain of dry grass and emptiness as far as the eye can see – an ochre steppe full of ruined trading posts overrun by beasts, smugglers and bandits.

## After Rogamon

The Border Fields belonged to the Free Alliance of Atania, which had little interest in them. When the Tralian Empire reached south and occupied parts of the fields, Atania left them to the Empire – in exchange for the promise that it would advance no further south.

## Present

For a long time the Empire tried to make something of the Border Fields, until it turned out they are almost worthless and too hostile to use. Today they are mostly a thorn in the Empire's side, lying across the most important trade roads to the south. Imperial influence barely reaches past the first miles of steppe; smuggling forbidden and stolen goods into Atania thrives.

The Border Fields are one of the few places in the Empire where mages are not actively hunted – some have settled here, preferring bandits to the Grey Guard. See [Tralian Empire](lore.html?realm=tralian-empire#realms).
