One of the richest cities of Varland, set on a cliff of the white rocks of the Crab Arm. The valuable white stone quarried nearby is made into marble and exported all over the world. White houses, clean streets, happy citizens – and jewellers whose unique pieces everyone of standing wants to own.

About 12,000 people live here, mostly wealthy merchants, nobles and respected craftsmen. Weißstein is known as an open city where many peoples live together; Durag are especially welcome for their bond with stone and earth.

## Before Rogamon

Founded to quarry marble and rare minerals from the cliffs of the Crab Arm, Weißstein quickly became a centre of craft and trade.

## Rogamon's Reign

One of the few cities **completely spared** by King Rogamon.

## After Rogamon

*Placeholder.* What Weißstein was like after the fall of Rogamon – here the province is a de facto kingdom of its own.

**Later in this era:** One of the few cities that practises magic openly again after Rogamon – real mages are still rare, but the fear of magic is much smaller here. The Inverlin family has served the crown as governors for about 150 years.

## Present

Ruled by **Lord Inverlin**, whose title is hereditary. Together with Westkliff it governs Weißküst, with Weißstein sending the larger share of envoys – and its governor sits on the council of the [Red-White Alliance](lore.html?realm=red-white-alliance#realms).
