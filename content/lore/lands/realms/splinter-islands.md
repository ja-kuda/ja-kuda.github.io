Not really a land of its own but countless islands, atolls and archipelagos in the Evening Sea, off the south-west of the continent. There is no single government: the **Ivory League**, an alliance of powerful merchant guilds and island towns, claims to rule, while pirate fleets fight over the islands – often every island is held by a different group.

## Present

Many pirate crews have sworn themselves to **Iron's Crossing**, a league protecting their freedom under a pirate king; others sail independently.
