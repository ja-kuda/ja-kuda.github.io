Once one of the largest provinces of Varland. Tralien is split into many smaller areas – **Central Tralien**, the **Stahlmark** and more – and is rarely seen as one province; the cultural and geographical differences are too great.

## Before Rogamon

One of the largest provinces of the great kingdom of Varland.

## After Rogamon

After Rogamon's end a movement arose here against any use of magic "for the protection of the kingdom". It quickly grew into the **Tralian movement**.

## Unification

The Tralian movement became the foundation of the [Tralian Empire](lore.html?realm=tralian-empire#realms).

## Present

The heartland of the Empire.
