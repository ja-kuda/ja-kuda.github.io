The **Summerlight Coast** (Sommerlichtküste) lies in the south-west of the continent. The river **Gamberi** separates it from the realms of the Suncourt Basin. Its rulers, the **Sea Lords**, keep order with the **Gold Chains** – mercenary companies recognisable by the golden chains on their armour and weapons. See [Factions](#factions).
