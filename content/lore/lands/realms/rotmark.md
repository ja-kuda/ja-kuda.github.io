The march in the south, framed by natural barriers: the Endwasser in the north, a moor in the west (see [Stillmarsch](lore.html?realm=stillmarsch#realms)), the Rotmark Mountains, the Border Mountains and the Blue Arm in the south. Its name comes from the many battles fought on its fields – grassy plains soaked in blood, shallow graves circled by crows.

The larger towns – **Steilfurt**, Mondburg and Windwart – are all heavily fortified. Most inhabitants work for the military in some way; they are hardened, willing to sacrifice, and have no patience for idle talk. Norvos, Debilor and Baros are worshipped most.

**Steilfurt** guards one of the very few crossings of the Blue Arm and is called the most strategically important fortress on the continent.

## Before Rogamon

Always contested: when humans arrived and drove out the other peoples, and later when the human realms fought each other, there was fighting in the Rotmark.

## After Rogamon

*Placeholder.* What Rotmark was like after the fall of Rogamon – here the province is a de facto kingdom of its own.

## Unification

The main training ground and base of Varland's armies. The last war between the Empire and Varland ended here, when Tralian troops trying to cross the Blue Arm to take Steilfurt were crushed on the *Plains of Decision*. A truce followed; since then the Rotmark has been Varland's only land link to the rest of the continent.

## Present

Ruled by the military. Parts of the south are now **occupied** by the Tralian army. Together with Weißküst the Rotmark has formed the [Red-White Alliance](lore.html?realm=red-white-alliance#realms); the governor of Steilfurt sits on its council.
