**Central Tralien** – the central part of the former Varland province and one of the most densely settled parts of the Empire. Perfect climate for wine and fruit, wealthy towns and citizens. Along the Blue Arm, the border to Varland and the Rotmark, it has had its share of war – but a little further away lie wide plains, clear lakes and rivers and small woods. Hardly anyone goes hungry.

The **Ark Tradistionala** is strongest here – a constant thorn in the side of the imperial government.
