A rocky island off the coast of Silbertal that rises from the Evening Sea like a giant iceberg – the last bastion of the **Durag**. Its capital **Brung**, *the great cauldron*, lies inside an extinct volcano: a city of rings spiralling ever deeper into the mines, each ring with its own purpose – living, smithing, administration, farming, mining, faith. Daylight reaches only the upper rings; below, light comes from glowing fungi, magic lamps and even lava.

About 300,000 people live on Bregonia, nearly all of them Durag – conservative, distrustful of strangers and still holding a grudge against humans after more than 2,000 years. Hard work, family and always time for a mug of mead define their culture. The **Church of Grumlin** dominates; other gods are barely tolerated. Stone wyverns, trolls and earth elementals roam outside the safe paths; ghosts and ancient constructs haunt the depths.

## Present

After the Expansion broke away, the Durag too rejected the nominal rule of King Anselm I and returned to the old ways: a Durag kingdom reborn under the current **Shield Lord**, head of one of the old Durag houses, who rules hard but fair.
