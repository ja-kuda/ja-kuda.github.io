The kingdom of the north, once the greatest realm of the continent. Shortly after the first accord with Atania it was divided into regions, each under a lord who could set its own laws while remaining subject to the crown.

## Before Rogamon

A great kingdom ruled by the arcane nobility, for centuries one of the two great powers of the continent next to Atania. See [Before Rogamon](#before-rogamon).

## During Rogamon's reign

Conquered by the Necromancer King – see [During Rogamon's reign](#rogamons-reign).

## Unification

Several northern provinces join together and Varland has a king again; the kingdom as it is known today takes shape. Its border wars with the Tralian Empire end on the Plains of Decision.

## Present

The remnants of the great kingdom are only a shadow of themselves. King **Anselm I** rules as liege lord, ever more centralised and withdrawn. When it became clear after Rogamon's second destruction that the Tralian Empire would invade, Anselm – out of fear and distrust of his vassals – cast off the outer provinces that would feel the invasion first and gathered all military power in the Partition around **Hammerfall**.

That many of the richest and militarily most important provinces were abandoned, and that inside the shrunken kingdom its own fight for survival rages, the king accepted in his fear. See [The present](#present).
