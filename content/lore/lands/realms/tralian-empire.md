A strongly militaristic empire in the centre of the continent that forbids or heavily restricts every form of magic. It is ruled centrally from **Goldzahn** on the gold-rich island of **Atikun** – heavily fortified, imperial, extremely rich, with academies and a university. The **Emperor** stands at the top, with the **Government Council** of his most influential administrators and advisers directly below him; the council has limited veto rights, but would be powerless against an emperor who truly seized unlimited power.

Cities and regions are run by **regents**, often high-ranking officers; in remote areas they have considerable autonomy. In peacetime, soldiers form most of the city guards. Mages found by the **Grey Guard** are drafted into the army or killed.

Regions: **Atikun**, **Tralien** (Central Tralien, Stahlmark …), the **Tralian Wilderness** in the east (only nominally controlled), the **Border Fields** (dry steppe, smugglers and beasts – one of the few places in the Empire where mages are not actively hunted) and **South Parnast**.

## After Rogamon

After Tralien separated from Varland, the new Empire quickly conquered parts of the centre of the continent and became one of the three great powers. See [After Rogamon](#after-rogamon) for the list of emperors.

## Present

Ruled by **Emperor Suta**, who has launched a great offensive against Varland. See [The present](#present).
