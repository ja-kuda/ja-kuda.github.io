A valley in the west, hemmed in between the **Silberling** and the **Brachtung**. At first glance quiet: calm forests, small villages, picturesque streams. Look closer and you see abandoned villages, fallow fields and derelict mines – late consequences of the Necromancer King and the economic crisis that followed.

The deeper woods are haunted by magical plant creatures and – legend says – witches (see [Hexenwald](lore.html?realm=hexenwald#realms)). Dragons are said to live on the peaks, though they haven't shown themselves for generations. Saranah, Mondarn and Norvos are worshipped most.

**Later in this era:** The valley recovered only slowly and its mines seemed to fail. New mines brought a short silver rush – then it ran dry, and a crisis followed. The town of **Kamrum** became a ghost town on the shore of the Twins, **Kilrum** lost most of its people, and rule fell back to the crown.

## Before Rogamon

Named for its many silver deposits, the valley drew determined merchants and ambitious miners.

## Rogamon's Reign

Scene of several rebellions, all crushed. Rogamon occupied most of the valley and carried as much silver as possible to Winterglehn. Ruined fortresses and abandoned villages remain to this day.

## Present

Mostly farmers and older people live here; the young want to leave. The valley is split between many minor lords and small groups. Together with the Expansion it now forms the [New Expansion](lore.html?realm=new-expansion#realms).
