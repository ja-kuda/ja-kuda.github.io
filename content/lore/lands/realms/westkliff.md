A harbour city on the southern west coast, built where the cliffs of the northern Crab Arm grow lower and passable. Its seaward side is lined with watchtowers, strong walls and defences. In the north of the city lie the dry docks where most of the ships of Varland's fleet are built and repaired; lumber camps outside the walls supply the wood.

About 25,000 people live in the city and its lands – mostly soldiers and fighters, merchants and craftsmen. Every citizen is ready to take up arms. Debilor and Norvos have the most followers; the great temple of Norvos stands in the city centre.

## Rogamon's Reign

Westkliff was attacked again and again and slowly worn down and destroyed.

## Present

Westkliff has a lord – currently **Mastor Donnfall** – but is run like a fortress: the real power lies with the military under **Major Klimmfurt**. Its governor is one of the three who lead the [Red-White Alliance](lore.html?realm=red-white-alliance#realms).
