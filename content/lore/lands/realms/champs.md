**Champ de Soleil**, better known to most Tralians as *the city where no one stays*. Nearly every merchant and traveller passes through it on the way to the heart of the Empire, or to the east of Atania without crossing the Border Fields. It lies between the three great cities of Central Tralien, never stands still, and only few people live there permanently.

It is famous for its inns, taverns, brothels and every other kind of lodging and entertainment. Its hospitality has limits: Orvosa, Korna and other "exotic" peoples are unwelcome here – more than anywhere else in the Empire – and may even face open hostility.

Business is booming: large groups of mercenaries and soldiers pass through and spend their last pay before marching north. Part of the [Tralian Empire](lore.html?realm=tralian-empire#realms).
