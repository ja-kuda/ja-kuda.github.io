**The Partition** – the central region of Varland. It divides the kingdom in two: in the north the **Schnitter** mountains split the continent, in the south the **Endwasser** is a natural barrier between west and east. Mountains, great rivers, waterfalls and hill plains – and in the north-east the crystal fields of [the Scars](lore.html?realm=belt#realms).

The capital **Hammerfall** stands on a height between two arms of the Endwasser, with the king's palace right at the top, above the waterfalls. The south is farmland. Debilor and Norvos are worshipped most, but on Hammerfall's temple square every god has a place.

The Partition is also the seat of Varland's **necromancer hunters**, founded after the age of necromancy to find and destroy the dark magic.

## Before Rogamon

Hammerfall was founded at the same time as Beltheim and soon became the capital of the young kingdom. The two cities quarrelled over which should be the capital – for some decades it was even Beltheim – until a later king made Hammerfall the seat of the crown again.

## Rogamon's Reign

Hammerfall resisted the necromancer and his armies for a long time, but finally fell through a trick of the new ruler. After its fall the remaining great centres of resistance broke.

## Present

King **Anselm I** has pulled all military power back into the Partition and around Hammerfall, casting off the outer provinces. Without the guidance of the Inquisition the old king seems overwhelmed and clings to the hope that the abandoned provinces will slow the Empire long enough.
