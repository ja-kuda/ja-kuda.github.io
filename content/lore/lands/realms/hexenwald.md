A huge, extremely dense forest near Kilrum at the foot of the Silberling, full of different trees, plants and animals.

Since an accident in a nearby mine it is considered cursed and is filled with magic that breaks out at irregular intervals – animals mutate, or mages become especially strong and don't tire. As the name suggests, the forest is said to be home to **witches** and dark creatures under their control. True or not, it has a magical aura, and those who venture deep enough will meet some of its magical inhabitants.

Workers and adventurers once lived at its edges; today there are only a few secluded settlements.
