**Beltheim and the Scars.** The Scars in the north-east of the Partition are a land of magically charged pink crystals. Legend says they were made in the last of the God Wars, in a battle between two gods now unknown. Gorges and cracks full of crystals mark the plains before **Beltheim**, a city built at the foot of – and largely into – a sheer cliff, overlooked by an elven fortress said to be cursed.

The crystals amplify any magic around them and make it unstable. To prevent accidents the people of Beltheim live in a heavily supervised and organised society – newcomers and travellers are often overwhelmed by its bureaucracy.

## Before Rogamon

People saw the potential of the crystals and began to mine them, with devastating accidents. To reduce the danger from new cracks in the ground, most of the city was built into the cliff. For some decades Beltheim was even the capital of Varland.

## Rogamon's Reign

Rogamon spared Beltheim at first – he wanted the crystals' energy and needed those who knew how to handle them. Once he had forged magic weapons and armour for his armies, he **wiped out the entire population** so the crystals could not be used against him.

**Later in this era:** Beltheim slowly recovered from the loss of its people and resumed the long work of mining the crystals – the right hand of the crown in governing the Partition.

## Present

The Scars are governed by **High Inquisitor Horuas**, appointed by King Loric. Any magic is forbidden in Beltheim and harshly punished by the Inquisition and its **Arcane Guard**. The region remains part of the [Kingdom of Varland](lore.html?realm=varland#realms).
