# Lands and Realms

For centuries Akudia was divided between two great realms – the **Kingdom of Varland** in the north and the **Free Alliance of Atania** in the south. Rogamon's reign destroyed this order. After his fall the provinces splintered into de facto kingdoms, the **Tralian Empire** rose in the centre of the continent, and Varland was rebuilt – only to fall apart again in the present war.

> [!glance] The realms today
> | | |
> |---|---|

> | **Free Alliance of Atania** | City-states in the south, held together by trade and old accords |
> | **Red-White Alliance** | Weißküst and the Rotmark, united against the Empire |
> | **New Expansion** | The Expansion and Silbertal under a nameless warlord, capital Zweigheim |
> | **Bregonia** | The reborn kingdom of the Durag under the Shield Lord |
> | **Splinter Islands** | Countless islands ruled by merchant guilds and pirates |
