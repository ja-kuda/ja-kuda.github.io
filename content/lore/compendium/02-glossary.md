# Glossary

Names and terms of the world, sorted by topic. Add a row to add a term – keep each list alphabetical.

> [!note]
> Game terms like checks, threads as a resource or talents are explained in the [System](system.html#key-terms).

## Time and history

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ascension** | The moment the gods left the world and became pure ideas – see [The Ascension](#ascension) |
| **Day of Decision** | Tralian day of remembrance for the Empire's defeat on the Plains of Decision |
| **Elf Wars** | The war of the dying elves against the humans – the greatest war since the age of the gods – see [Before Rogamon](#before-rogamon) |
| **Fields of Silence** | The former Golden Valley, drained of all life by Rogamon; it lies in eternal dusk – see [Rogamon's Reign](#rogamons-reign) |
| **First Accord** | The treaty that divided the continent between Varland and Atania |
| **God Wars** | The wars between the gods that shattered reality into the mirror realities |
| **Golden Times** | The long age of growth and prosperity before necromancy appeared |
| **nKR** | "After the King's reign" – Varland's calendar, counting from Rogamon's fall (year 0) |
| **Plains of Decision** | Battlefield on the Blue Arm near Steilfurt where the Tralian army was crushed |
| **Second Accord** | The treaty that gave Atania's city-states their self-government and made it the Free Alliance |
| **Shame of Norvall** | The night King Sigmar had his own city burned rather than surrender it to Rogamon |
| **The Tearing** | The moment the Null tore open and the weave appeared |
| **Tralian movement** | The movement against all magic "for the protection of the kingdom" that grew into the Tralian Empire |
| **vKR** | "Before the King's reign" – years before Rogamon's fall in Varland's calendar |

## Faith and magic

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Aestor** | The god who gave up his ascension to guard the border between life and death – see [The gods](#gods) |
| **Ambition** | The deepest ideal that drives a person – and the idea each god became when they ascended |
| **Arcane nobility** | The old mage families who ruled much of Varland before Rogamon |
| **Arcane scars** | Marks left on weavers who pull too many threads; they always show the weaver's element – see [Weaving](#weaving) |
| **Arkas** | A god who killed himself to escape his ascension; since then far more beings are born with arcane gifts |
| **Blights** | Diseases and curses created by the elves to rule the other peoples, later sealed away |
| **Blind Seer** | A dark god banished by Aestor, who whispered to King Sigmar – see [The gods](#gods) |
| **Corvilas** | God of corruption and once ruler of the elves, blamed for their fall – see [The gods](#gods) |
| **Elements** | The building blocks of the fabric of reality that weavers reshape – see [The elements](#elements) |
| **Fabric of reality** | What shields existence from the Null – see [The fabric of reality and the Null](#fabric) |
| **Feywild** | Another world, reached through rifts that open regularly near Feitor |
| **Inferna** | Goddess of emotion who freed the Orvosa from Orvos |
| **Limbo** | The space between life and death created by Aestor |
| **Mirror realities** | The shards reality broke into during the God Wars |
| **Necromancy** | Weaving with the Life element – souls, the dead and the undead – see [Necromancy](#necromancy) |
| **Null** | What lies behind the fabric of reality – see [The fabric of reality and the Null](#fabric) |
| **Orvos** | The god who created the Orvosa as will-less servants; killed by Inferna |
| **Rift** | A tear in the fabric of reality – see [Weaving, threads and their risks](#weaving) |
| **Static magic** | Scholarly magic with fixed, "encapsulated" spells that only borrow threads – see [Static magic](#static-magic) |
| **Threads** | Strands of the fabric pulled loose by arcane weavers |
| **The Veil** | Aestor's shield between the world and Limbo |
| **Visaron the Seer** | The god whose death tore the border between life and death and brought necromancy into the world |
| **Weave** | The mass of energies and threads exposed when the Null tore open; the fabric of reality is woven from it – see [The fabric of reality and the Null](#fabric) |
| **Weaver** | An arcane mage who reshapes the fabric of reality freely |

## Peoples and persons

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Anselm I** | The present king of Varland, who cast off the outer provinces |
| **Darion Sterling** | Leader of the Rightwalkers |
| **Durag** | The people of stone and earth, made in Grumlin's image – today mostly found on Bregonia – see [Peoples](#peoples) |
| **Elves** | The extinct first rulers of Akudia; only ruins and legends remain |
| **High Inquisitor Horuas** | Ruler of Beltheim and the Scars on behalf of the king |
| **Korna** | A once warlike people, nearly wiped out by humans and exiled to the far north – see [Peoples](#peoples) |
| **Lartarte** | "The Mage" – the emperor who loosened the persecution of mages in the Tralian Empire |
| **Lord of Tears** | The father of necromancy and Rogamon's teacher – see [Legends](#legends) |
| **Orvosa** | The people created by the god Orvos – see [Peoples](#peoples) |
| **Pirate King Sulden** | Leader of the Brotherhood of Irons Crossing |
| **Rabenhorst** | Lord of southern Triften who ended Rogamon's reign with the 13 Faithful |
| **Rogamon** | The Necromancer King whose reign plunged the continent into darkness – see [History](#history) |
| **Santor** | "The Awakened Lion" – emperor before Suta, who vanished |
| **Shield Lord** | The ruler of Bregonia, head of one of the old Durag houses |
| **Sigmar** | King of Norvall and Rogamon's great enemy – remembered for the Shame of Norvall |
| **Suta** | "The Bearer of Hope" – the present emperor of the Tralian Empire |

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Akudia** | The continent on which Ambition takes place |
| **Ars Arkanum** | The academy of magic in Winters End – see [Magic in everyday life](#everyday-magic) |
| **Atania** | The Free Alliance of Atania, a league of city-states in the south |
| **Beltheim** | City built into a cliff above the Scars, ruled by the Inquisition |
| **Blue Arm** | The river that forms the border between Varland and the Tralian Empire |
| **Border Fields** | Dry steppe in the south of the Empire – smugglers, bandits and mages on the run |
| **Bregonia** | The rocky island and reborn kingdom of the Durag, ruled by the Shield Lord from Brung |
| **Brung** | "The great cauldron" – the Durag capital inside an extinct volcano |
| **Driftholz** | A ruined town in the north of the Fields of Silence |
| **Expansion** | The trade region in Varland's north-west around Winterglehn; today part of the New Expansion |
| **Goldzahn** | Capital of the Tralian Empire on the island of Atikun |
| **Hammerfall** | Capital of Varland, above the waterfalls of the Endwasser |
| **Ice Desert** | The frozen far north of the continent |
| **New Expansion** | Present-day realm of the Expansion and Silbertal under a nameless warlord – capital Zweigheim |
| **Partition** | The heartland of Varland around Hammerfall |
| **Red-White Alliance** | Present-day alliance of the Weißküst towns and Rotmark |
| **Salt Road** | One of the most important trade roads of the continent, from Winterglehn into Varland |
| **The Scars** | The crystal region around Beltheim, where magic grows strong and unstable |
| **Splinter Islands** | Countless islands in the Evening Sea, fought over by merchant guilds and pirates |
| **Steilfurt** | Fortress city at one of the few crossings of the Blue Arm |
| **Tralian Empire** | The anti-magic empire in the centre of the continent, ruled by Emperor Suta |
| **Varland** | The old great kingdom of the north, capital Hammerfall |
| **Weißküst** | The rich south-west coast of Varland with its white cliffs |
| **Winterglehn** | The largest city of the continent and once Rogamon's capital |
| **Winters End** | Town in the far north where the weave is strongest; home of the Ars Arkanum |

| **Cabal of Korvus** | A dark faction; its remnants now follow the Raven – see [Factions](#factions) |

| **Conclave** | A secret circle with ties to Rogamon – see [Factions](#factions) |
| **First Answer** | A guild of mercenary healers who save their paying clients at any cost |
| **Gold Chains** | Mercenary companies serving the Sea Lords of the Summerlight Coast |
| **Grey Guard** | The Tralian Empire's mage hunters |
| **Ice Warriors** | A war band of Korna, half-giants and others in the Ice Desert |
| **Inquisition** | Rules Beltheim and the Scars and forbids all magic there |
| **Ivory League** | Alliance of merchant guilds and towns that claims to govern the Splinter Islands |
| **Machinarium** | A secretive order within the Tralian Empire – see [Factions](#factions) |
| **Necromancer hunters** | Varland's guild for tracking down necromancy, founded after Rogamon |
| **Paladins of the Good** | Lord Rabenhorst's resistance against Rogamon |
| **Raiders** | Seafaring bands from the Ice Desert who raid Varland's northern coasts |
| **Raven who cleanses the world** | A mysterious being worshipped by the remnants of the Cabal of Korvus – see [Legends](#legends) |
| **Rightwalkers** | Adventurers who right injustices and help the poor |
| **Sea Lords** | The rulers of the Summerlight Coast in Atania |
