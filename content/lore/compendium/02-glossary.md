# Glossary

Add a row to add a term – keep the list alphabetical.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Aestor** | The god who gave up his ascension to guard the border between life and death – see [The gods](#gods) |
| **Akudia** | The continent on which Ambition takes place |
| **Arcane Guard** | The Inquisition's guard in Beltheim, where using magic is a crime |
| **Ars Arkanum** | The academy of magic – see [Magic in everyday life](#everyday-magic) |
| **Ascension** | The moment the gods left the world to become pure faith and power – see [The Ascension](#ascension) |
| **Atania** | The Free Alliance of Atania, a league of city-states in the south |
| **Bregonia** | The reborn kingdom of the Durag, ruled by the Shield Lord from Brung |
| **Cabal of Korvus** | A dark faction; its remnants now follow the Raven – see [Factions](#factions) |
| **Conclave** | Secret circle working to bring Rogamon back – see [Factions](#factions) |
| **Durag** | The people of stone and earth, made in Grumlin's image – today mostly found on Bregonia. See [Peoples](#peoples) |
| **Fabric of reality** | What shields existence from the Null – see [The fabric of reality](#fabric) |
| **God Wars** | The wars between the gods that shattered reality into the mirror realities |
| **Grey Guard** | The Tralian Empire's mage hunters |
| **Limbo** | The space between life and death created by Aestor |
| **Lord of Tears** | The father of necromancy and Rogamon's teacher – see [Legends](#legends) |
| **Machinarium** | Hidden power behind the Tralian throne – see [Factions](#factions) |
| **Mirror realities** | The shards reality broke into during the God Wars |
| **nKR** | "After the King's reign" – Varland's calendar, counting from Rogamon's fall (year 0) |
| **Null** | What lies behind the fabric of reality – see [The Null](#null) |
| **Orvosa** | The people created by the god Orvos – see [Peoples](#peoples) |
| **Paladins of the Good** | Lord Rabenhorst's resistance against Rogamon |
| **Rabenhorst** | Lord of southern Triften who ended Rogamon's reign with the 13 Faithful |
| **Red-White Alliance** | Present-day alliance of the Weißküst towns and Rotmark |
| **Rift** | A tear in the fabric of reality – see [The Rifts](#rifts) |
| **Rogamon** | The Necromancer King whose reign plunged the continent into darkness – see [History](#history) |
| **The Scars** | The crystal region around Beltheim, where magic grows strong and unstable |
| **The Tearing** | The moment the Null tore open and the weave appeared |
| **Threads** | Strands of the fabric pulled loose by arcane weavers |
| **Tralian Empire** | The empire in the centre of the continent, ruled by Emperor Suta |
| **The Veil** | Aestor's shield between the world and Limbo |
| **Varland** | The old great kingdom of the north, capital Hammerfall |
| **Weave** | The mass of energies and threads exposed when the Null tore open; the fabric of reality is woven from it – see [The fabric of reality](#fabric) |
