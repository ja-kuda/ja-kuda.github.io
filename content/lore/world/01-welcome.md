# Welcome to Akudia

**Akudia** is a land scarred by gods, elves and a Necromancer King – and now torn apart by war. For centuries the continent lay under the rule of **Rogamon**, who raised the dead to free the common people from their lords and ended up as a tyrant. His fall left the provinces splintered. Some of them have since united again, but today the anti-magic **Tralian Empire** marches north against what is left of the Kingdom of **Varland**.

## Five things everyone in Akudia knows

1. **Rogamon is the name of fear.** The Necromancer King ruled for centuries. His undead, his ruined lands and the Fields of Silence are still there – and some say he has risen again.
2. **War has come.** The Tralian Empire wants to put an end to magic once and for all. Varland's king has abandoned his outer provinces, and the borderlands must defend themselves.
3. **Magic is power – and danger.** Mages once ruled Varland's nobility. In the Tralian Empire they are hunted by the Grey Guard. Necromancers are feared everywhere.
4. **The gods are distant.** They no longer act in the world, but people still pray to them – each realm to its own.
5. **The elves are gone.** Once they ruled Akudia; today only ruins, curses and dark rumours remain of them.
