# Faiths and Gods

The gods of Akudia were once mortal beings – the first who learned to sense and command the weave. As their followers grew in number and their faith grew stronger, they rose to become the rulers of the young world. In the end they **ascended**: they turned into abstract forms of their own ambitions. Since then the ascended gods are the true gods of reality, but they can no longer act directly upon the world they once ruled.

> [!glance] At a glance
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **The gods** | Former rulers of the world who ascended and became ideas |
> | **Can they act?** | Not directly – they live on in the minds and faith of mortals |
> | **The exception** | Aestor, who gave up his ascension to guard the border between life and death |

## Faith across Akudia

| Region | Most worshipped gods |
|---|---|
| **Hammerfall and the Partition** | Debilor and Norvos – but the temple square of Hammerfall has a place for every god |
| **Rotmark** | Norvos, Debilor and Baros |
| **Westkliff** | Debilor and Norvos – the great temple of Norvos stands in the city centre |
| **Silbertal** | Saranah, Mondarn and Norvos |
| **The Expansion** | Almost every god has followers here |
| **Orubus** | Aleandra above all, also Baros and Buko |
| **Bregonia** | Grumlin – other gods are barely tolerated |
| **Tralian Empire** | Debilor is the main faith; Aestor, Mondarn, Saranah and Norvos are tolerated. Public worship and temples are not allowed. |
