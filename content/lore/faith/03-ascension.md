# The Ascension

> [!note]
> The age of the gods lies thousands of years in the past. What follows is how scholars and priests tell it today.

## The Tearing

Before life and structure existed, there was only the Null. One day the Null tore open and exposed the **weave** – a mass of energies and threads that began to form structures and existence. With these structures, reality as we know it was created.

Over thousands upon thousands of years, more and more complex and intelligent beings came into being. Many of the first are long forgotten, replaced again and again by new creatures. A few of them were special: they could sense the weave behind existence. Fewer still could communicate with it – and so they learned to control parts of the weave and achieve fantastic, unthinkable things.

## The birth of the gods

The more these beings learned about the weave, the stronger their ambitions became. Some gathered followers who supported them in exchange for protection and gifts. Soon some of them saw themselves as exalted, the rightful rulers of the young reality. They decided how their followers had to serve them; those who refused were cast out or killed. Many developed unnaturally long lives and began to call their followers *the mortals*.

Over time the word **gods** took hold among their followers – those who have the right and the power to order chaos and rule the mortals.

## Creation and calling

The more powerful the newborn gods became, the stronger their desire to make their ambitions real. Some reshaped the world, others changed the beings that lived in it – and so created the elves, the Durag and the humans. Others followed darker goals and tried to destroy or corrupt the creations of other gods.

The elves and Durag were the first, the Korna came a little later. Hundreds of years later the first humans appeared, far away from the other peoples – perhaps the secret experiment of an unknown god.

Again and again the gods clashed, and again and again the mortals suffered. Small groups joined into alliances that gave their faith to one god, hoping for protection – which only increased the gods' power and ambition. The gods were reduced more and more to a small part of their personality, which they lived out to the extreme.

The gods also noticed something else: the greatest way to change reality is the **death of a god**. When one of them died, it released a wave that shook the weave so strongly that laws of nature changed or parts of reality transformed in an instant.

## The God Wars

For centuries the gods created and manipulated reality. Conflicts grew, followers waged war on each other, and more and more of them turned to the most extreme aspects of their gods. In the end only one way remained for the cycle to end.

The **God Wars** were a series of conflicts so gigantic that whole ranks of gods were destroyed. The weave and the fabric of reality woven from it were shaken so badly that reality broke into thousands of shards – the birth of the **mirror realities**, the most important event in the cosmos since the Tearing. The Orvosa and other peoples were created and destroyed, new forms of magic found their way into the realities, and rifts between the mirror worlds opened and closed, taking hundreds of thousands of lives.

When **Visaron the Seer**, a god fascinated by the separation of life and death, was defeated and killed by an alliance of the other gods, he tore the border between life and death as he died – and brought necromancy into reality. **Aestor** took it upon himself to rebuild this border and began to weave **the Veil**.

## The Ascension

While the gods fought and their followers struggled to survive, the fanatical faith that fed the great gods grew so strong that they began to detach from reality. Too late they realised that their search for endless power pulled them ever closer to their strongest convictions. First they lost their unstable forms, then their power over the shattered reality, until they blurred into abstract concepts – more idea than being.

This is the **Ascension**. Some gods welcomed it, as they could now be everywhere for their followers. Others did not – and used their last power to change the world one final time, regardless of the cost:

- **Corvilas**, once the god of the elves, tried to destroy as many of his followers as possible to avoid his ascension. He failed, because elven souls cannot be destroyed – and swore revenge from his exile.
- **Arkas** killed himself instead of accepting his fate, and with his death changed the weave: since then, more and more beings are born with arcane abilities.
- **Orvos** ordered all Orvosa, his creations, to attack the humans at once. Before they were wiped out, **Inferna**, the goddess of emotion and love, broke his control, killed the corrupt god and helped the Orvosa win enough victories to break free. Humans have seen Inferna ever since as the one who sided with the demons of a dark god.

With the Ascension the God Wars ended, and soon the secrets of gaining divine power were lost among the mortals. The world lay largely devastated – only a few places, such as Akudia, still held life.
