# Peoples

Akudia was once the home of the **elves**, the **Durag** and the **Korna**. When **humans** arrived about two thousand years ago, they settled and conquered the continent piece by piece. The elves lost their immortality and vanished from the world, the Korna were nearly wiped out and fled into exile in the far north, and the Durag withdrew deep underground or to the island of **Bregonia**. Rule over the continent fell to the humans.

Today humans make up most of the population everywhere. In the realms of Akudia the peoples live together in relative peace – with notable exceptions:

- In **Varland**, especially in the Expansion and in cities like Weißstein, people of all origins live side by side.
- In the **Tralian Empire**, Hinan and Durag are treated as ordinary citizens. The **Orvosa**, **Korna** and other "exotic" peoples are merely tolerated, often avoided, paid less or refused work – many believe they carry magic and the devil inside them.
- On **Bregonia**, almost everyone is a Durag. Many Durag are conservative, dislike strangers and still hold a grudge against humans after more than two thousand years – for many it is now simply tradition.
- In the **Ice Desert**, nomadic tribes roam – among them Korna clans whose ancestors survived the persecution.

> [!note] The elves
> The elves are extinct. They live on only in history, in legends and in the ruins they left behind – see [Before Rogamon](#before-rogamon).
